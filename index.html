About us

Independent by design.

NorthForks Innovations, LLC is an independently owned company based in Grand Forks, North Dakota. We create clear, respectful, and practical digital and physical products for everyday use.

Our work begins with a simple question: can thoughtful design make a useful idea easier to understand and put into practice?

TG

Founder

Tiram Gamliel, LPCC

Managing Member

NorthForks Innovations was founded by Tiram Gamliel, a licensed professional clinical counselor with more than two decades of experience helping people work through difficult moments. The company brings that human-centered perspective to practical digital and physical products.
