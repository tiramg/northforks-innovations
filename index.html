Our approach

From a useful observation to a considered product.

Notice the problem

Start with a specific frustration or unmet need found in everyday life.

Make it approachable

Reduce unnecessary complexity and build around what people genuinely need.

Improve through use

Test carefully, listen to feedback, and refine the details that matter.

About us

Independent by design.

NorthForks Innovations, LLC is an independently owned software company based in Grand Forks, North Dakota. We create clear, respectful, and practical digital experiences for everyday use.

Our work begins with a simple question: can thoughtful design make a useful idea easier to understand and put into practice?

TG

Founder

Tiram Gamliel

Managing Member

NorthForks Innovations was founded by Tiram Gamliel, a licensed professional clinical counselor with more than two decades of experience helping people work through difficult moments. The company brings that human-centered perspective to the development of practical digital and physical products.
