About NorthForks

Different problems. One practical approach.

NorthForks Innovations, LLC is an independent product-development company based in Grand Forks, North Dakota. We develop practical ideas across different areas—from digital tools that support reflection and learning to physical products intended to make everyday tasks easier.

Our projects may look very different from one another, but they begin in the same place: noticing a real problem and asking whether there is a clearer, more useful way to solve it.
